• "Skip warm-up" and "Skip cool-down" are now separate settings, so you can skip one and keep the other. Your existing choice carries over.
• New button on the workout screen to skip a warm-up or cool-down while it is running.
• New option to turn off the second countdown warning and keep only the first.
• New Tones mode: chimes instead of a voice for interval changes, countdowns and the end of a workout. Settings → Cue type → Tones.
• Your weekly streak now also shows on the History screen.
• Interval changes can also buzz your smartwatch. Settings → Also notify smartwatch. Thanks to west1nat0r.
• Stop the same workout early twice and C2K suggests repeating the week before.
• Run, walk and completed-day colors are easier to read.
• Translation fixes.
